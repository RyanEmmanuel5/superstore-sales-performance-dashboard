# Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

The Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard is a data analytics project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It explores sales performance, profitability, order activity, and product performance using the Sample Superstore dataset.

The dashboard presents key performance indicators (KPIs) and interactive visualizations to help users understand business performance across different regions, customer segments, shipping modes, product categories, and time periods.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability.
- Identify top-performing product categories and regions.
- Examine customer segment contributions to sales.
- Evaluate product performance and yearly sales trends.
- Present business insights through interactive visualizations.

## Tools and Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Sample Superstore Dataset

## Key Performance Indicators

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity
- Average Order Value
- Profit Margin

## Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes:

- Sales by Customer Segment
- Sales by Product Category
- Sales by Region
- Profit by Product Category
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Sales by Year

Interactive filters are available for Region, Segment, Ship Mode, and Order Date.

## Key Business Insights

1. Technology recorded the highest sales among the product categories.
2. The West region generated the highest sales.
3. The Consumer segment contributed the most sales.
4. Technology recorded the highest total profit.
5. 2017 recorded the highest sales among the years shown.

## Business Recommendations

- Strengthen the Technology category through effective inventory management and targeted promotions.
- Maintain the West region's strong sales performance.
- Focus on customer engagement and retention within the Consumer segment.
- Monitor Technology product costs and profitability.
- Investigate the factors behind the strong sales performance in 2017.
